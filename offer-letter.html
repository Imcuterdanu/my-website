Dear Justin Mai:

Congratulations! You have been selected to receive a 2024 National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship. The Fellowship offers three years of support over a five-year period which will be paid through your academic institution.

NSF publishes the names of Fellowship Offers and Honorable Mentions at research.gov/grfp. Unless you indicated otherwise at the time of application, your name will be listed.

Please read the following information carefully and thoroughly to ensure that you understand the terms and requirements of the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship before accepting the fellowship. If you accept this offer, you are certifying to the National Science Foundation, a US federal agency, that you:

  1. Meet the eligibility requirements detailed in the GRF Program Solicitation. Intend to enroll or be enrolled for the entire period of the Fellowship, as a full-time student in a research-based graduate degree program in an eligible field of study at an accredited non-profit U.S. institution of higher education.
  2. Have read, understood, and accepted the terms and conditions of the Fellowship described in detail under Fellow Responsibilities in the GRFP Administrative Guide for Fellows and Coordinating Officials.
  3. Throughout the term of the Fellowship, you remain subject to all the requirements of the degree program in which you are enrolled as determined by your graduate institution.

The NSF Graduate Research Fellowship cannot be combined or held concurrently with another individual federal fellowship, nor can it be deferred if you are not enrolled in an eligible graduate program.

Although the Fellowship is offered to you, the funds are awarded to your academic institution which is responsible for paying your stipend. You are not the Principal Investigator of the NSF GRFP award nor an NSF employee, you are a full-time graduate student at an academic institution whose graduate education and training is being supported with NSF Graduate Research Fellowship funds.

The Fellowship provides three years of financial support within a five-year fellowship period or until you graduate (whichever comes first), comprising a stipend of $37,000 per twelve-month Fellowship Year in monthly allotments of $3,083 that cannot be prorated or divided into smaller increments. Your academic institution will receive a direct payment of $16,000 towards the Cost of Education in lieu of all required tuition and mandatory fees for each of the three years of Fellowship funding. The use of the Cost of Education payment is determined by the academic institution, not the Fellow. All financial queries about your Fellowship should be directed to your academic institution.

The reviews of your application will be available on April 15, 2024 in the GRFP application site. Please print or save a copy for your records.

To accept (or decline) the Fellowship, see the attached Instructions.

Being chosen as a NSF Graduate Research Fellow is a significant national accomplishment and places you among an elite group of Fellows, many of whom have gone on to distinguished careers in STEM or STEM education. We wish you similar success in your graduate studies and continued progress toward your aspirations throughout your career.
